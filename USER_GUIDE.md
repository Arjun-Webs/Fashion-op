# 🛍️ AURA Fashion AI — User & Feature Guide

Welcome to **AURA Fashion AI**, the ultimate next-generation luxury fashion marketplace where style meets artificial intelligence. AURA connects fashion lovers, trendsetters, style communities, and luxury brands in one interactive experience.

This guide explains every feature available on the website in simple, user-friendly language.

---

## 🌟 What Makes AURA Special?

Unlike standard online stores that just show a static list of products, AURA is a **living fashion network**. On AURA:
- You don't just buy clothes—you discover **style tribes** (like *Quiet Luxury*, *Techwear*, or *Sneakerheads*).
- You can shop directly from outfit photos shared by top fashion creators.
- Creators can test their posts with an **AI Virality Predictor** to see how popular their outfit will be before sharing it.
- You can track fashion trends on a **Trend Radar** before they hit mainstream stores.

---

## 🧭 Main Features & How to Use Them

### 1. 🎭 Role Switcher (Explore Different Perspectives)
At the top right of the navigation bar, you can toggle your experience between four roles:
- **Customer**: Shop products, save items to your wishlist, and browse social posts.
- **Influencer**: Create outfit posts, tag products, and check your style influence score.
- **Admin**: Add new luxury items to the catalog and manage the platform.
- **Moderator**: Manage content and review community interactions.

---

### 2. 👗 Luxury Fashion Boutique Catalog
Located under the **Store** tab, the boutique lets you discover curated luxury fashion pieces.

- **Style Categories**: Filter items by fashion aesthetics such as *Quiet Luxury*, *Techwear*, *Streetwear*, *Retro Y2K*, *Sneakers*, *Ethnic Fusion*, *Minimal*, and *Sustainable*.
- **Trend Badges**: Items feature status badges like `Trending Hot`, `Rising Fast`, `Popular Classic`, or `New Drop` so you always know what's in high demand.
- **Sustainability Index**: Every product displays an eco-rating score out of 100, showing its environmental score.
- **Wishlist & Cart**: Save your favorite items with a single heart click or add items directly to your cart with preferred sizes and colors.

---

### 3. 📸 Social Commerce Feed & Creator Stories
Located under the **Social Feed** tab, this is your fashion social network.

- **Creator Stories**: View daily style stories and quick outfit highlights from leading fashion influencers.
- **Shoppable Posts**: Browse outfit photos and videos. Every post includes **tagged products**—click on any item to view its details or buy it instantly!
- **Create Post & AI Virality Predictor**:
  - Click the **+ Create Outfit Post** button to share your look.
  - As you type your caption, add hashtags, and tag products, the **AI Virality Predictor** analyzes your post in real time!
  - It predicts your expected likes, shares, virality score, and gives you actionable tips (e.g., *"Add 2 more trending hashtags to increase reach by 35%"*).

---

### 4. 📈 Fashion Trend Radar
Located under the **Trend Radar** tab, this feature acts as a fashion forecast tool.

- **Trend Lifecycles**: Track emerging hashtags (e.g., `#QuietLuxury`, `#Techwear`) through 5 stages: *Origin Seed* $\rightarrow$ *Early Trend* $\rightarrow$ *Peak Popularity* $\rightarrow$ *Mainstream* $\rightarrow$ *Declining*.
- **Adoption Speed**: See how many fashion lovers are adopting a trend every single day.
- **Geographic Spread**: Discover where in the world a trend is blowing up first.

---

### 5. 👥 Style Communities (Subculture Hubs)
Located under the **Communities** tab, this section groups fashion lovers into subculture circles.

- **Explore Style Tribes**: Discover hubs like *Quiet Luxury Circle*, *Sneakerhead Vault*, *Techwear Syndicate*, and *Retro Y2K Aesthetic*.
- **Top Influencers & Products**: See who the leading trendsetters are in each community and which products are most popular within that specific style vibe.
- **Community Growth**: See which style subcultures are growing the fastest.

---

### 6. 🕸️ Interactive Style Map (Network Explorer)
Located under the **Network Map** tab, this visual tool maps out how fashion connects everything together.

- **Visual Connection Web**: See interactive nodes representing Shoppers, Creators, Clothes, Hashtags, and Style Communities.
- **Discover Connections**: Click on any person or product to see what style tribes they belong to, who follows them, and which products are frequently bought together.

---

### 7. 🤖 AI Personal Stylist & Recommendation Lab
Located under the **AI Stylist** tab, this tool helps you find clothes tailored to your exact taste.

- **4 Recommendation Modes**:
  1. **Hybrid Match**: Blends all style signals for the ultimate custom recommendation.
  2. **Influencer Favorites**: Recommends items loved by top fashion creators.
  3. **Trending Hot**: Shows items currently spiking across social media.
  4. **Network Discoveries**: Finds hidden gems based on your style circle.
- **Purchase Intent Meter**: Evaluates how well a product matches your unique style profile.

---

### 8. 📊 Executive Analytics Dashboard
Located under the **Analytics** tab, this dashboard gives a high-level view of platform activity.

- **Sales & Growth Metrics**: View total revenue, active fashion lovers, and virality rate.
- **Top Style Leaders**: Check out the top-ranked influencers driving fashion sales and trends.

---

### 9. ⚙️ Admin Control Panel
Located under the **Admin** tab (available when switched to the Admin role).

- **Add New Products**: Easily upload new fashion pieces with name, brand, price, photos, available sizes, colors, and sustainability scores.
- **Connect New Items**: Automatically link new items into the fashion network so shoppers immediately see them in recommendations.

---

### 10. 🛒 Cart Drawer & Instant Checkout Modal
- **Sliding Cart Drawer**: Easily review selected items, update quantities, choose sizes/colors, and view subtotal calculations.
- **Checkout & Receipt**: Fill in delivery details and receive an instant order confirmation with tracking number and celebration animations!

---
